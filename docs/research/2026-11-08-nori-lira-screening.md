# Nori and Lira name screening

Date: 2026-11-08. Status: preliminary product-name screen; neither name is
legally cleared or selected.

## Findings

| Name | Verified first-party uses | Assessment for this distribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nori | [Nori](https://www.usenori.ai/) markets cloud coding-agent infrastructure supporting Claude Code, Codex CLI and other agents. Separately, [Nori Git client](https://meetnori.app/) markets repository review, rebasing and pull-request workflows alongside IDEs and agents. | Two close collisions with our agent-management and code-review workflows. Recommend dropping Nori for practical differentiation. |
| Lira | [HelgeSverre/lira](https://github.com/helgesverre/lira) is a work-in-progress systems programming language implemented in Rust. [Lira AI](https://lira-ai.com/) markets a personal AI assistant for Mac and iPhone, including research, follow-ups and recurring tasks. | Also already used in software and AI. The examples found are less directly similar to our editor workflow than Nori's, but Lira is not an unclaimed name. Retaining it would require a more focused assessment. |

The comparative assessment is an inference from the products' own descriptions,
not a finding about their popularity or a determination of trademark infringement.
The products were not installed or independently tested; their websites and
repositories establish advertised use of the names.

## Scope and limits

This follow-up checks existing product use through official websites and source
repositories. No trademark-register search, registration-status verification,
domain availability check, or legal clearance was performed. The earlier
[name-screening report](2026-11-08-name-screening.md) records the distinction
between product-name collisions, trademark rights and copyright.

Recommendation: reject Nori because its existing uses closely overlap the
intended product. Treat Lira as a personal favorite requiring further screening,
not as an available alternative established by this research.
